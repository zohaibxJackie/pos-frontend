POS FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Q1. What is a mobile shop POS?

A mobile shop POS is software that helps manage smartphone sales, accessories inventory and customer payments.

Q2. What is the best POS for mobile stores?

The best POS for mobile stores should support IMEI tracking, accessories inventory and fast checkout. It should also manage phone models, storage variants and customer purchase records easily.

Q3. How does a mobile POS system work?

A mobile POS system records smartphone and accessories sales, updates inventory automatically and tracks IMEI numbers. It also generates reports to help store owners monitor sales and profits.

Q4. How to use a mobile shop POS?

You simply add phone models and accessories to the system, scan products during checkout and complete the sale. The POS automatically updates stock and stores customer purchase information.

Q5. How can customers make payment?

Customers can pay using cash, debit cards, credit cards or digital payment methods. The POS records every payment and generates a receipt for the customer instantly.
